Privacy Policy — Mathic

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Mathic is a free math puzzle game for Android, developed by AugmentAr. This policy explains what data is collected when you use the app.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Mathic has no user accounts, no login, no analytics SDK, and no backend server of our own.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Mathic displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, in accordance with Google's own privacy policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

Google Play Games

Mathic integrates Google Play Games Services for optional sign-in. If you sign in, Google handles your account data under its own privacy policy. Sign-in is entirely optional and not required to play.

Permissions

  • INTERNET — required to load ads and connect to Play Games.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — lets the app check connectivity before requesting ads.

No access to camera, microphone, contacts, location, or storage is requested.

Children

Mathic is rated for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Ad serving for users under 13 uses Google's family-safe ad policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and post the new version at the same URL.
